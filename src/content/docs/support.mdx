## Before you open an issue

1. Check the [FAQ](/faq/) and the troubleshooting sections of the [hardware](/install/hardware/#troubleshooting) and
   [Linux](/install/linux/#troubleshooting) install guides.
2. Make sure you run the [latest release](/download/).
3. Search the [existing issues](https://github.com/freesp16/freesp16/issues?q=is%3Aissue); add your details to a
   matching one instead of opening a new one.

## A good bug report includes

- The freesp16 version, and whether it runs on the hardware or on Linux (with your distribution).
- On the hardware: the instrument's firmware version, the boot menu version and the `bootmenu.log` file from the
  USB key.
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead. Exact steps help most.
- A photo or screenshot of the screen if something looks wrong.
- On Linux: the terminal output.

Please don't contact Pioneer DJ or AlphaTheta about freesp16; they don't support it.
