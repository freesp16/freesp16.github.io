## New compared to the stock software

freesp16 keeps the SP-16's workflow and opens its projects, and adds:

- **A modern interface at 60 fps:** a redesigned screen drawn by the SP-16's graphics chip, with smooth meters and
  playheads. The pads light up with every hit, the sequencer's included.
- **[Advanced motion control](#motion):** record knob and touch strip moves into the pattern, then view, zoom and
  redraw them per step.
- **[Advanced bypass](#track-menu-and-bypass):** switch playback, the amp envelope or an effect off and on with one
  tap, to compare.
- **[A better mixer](#mixer-and-routing):** wider strips with live meters under the faders and a compact send /
  master pair.
- **[Solo and smarter mutes](#mute-and-solo):** solo from the pads, a mute mode per track (stop the sound or the
  steps), mutes saved per pattern, and a status badge.
- **[Plug-in instruments (coming soon)](#plug-in-instruments-coming-soon):** a CLAP plug-in host for software
  instruments and effects.
- **Small things that add up:** touch strip REPEAT hits recorded onto the steps, an optional CPU meter, and the POWER
  button saves your work, unsaved changes included, and reopens it at the next start.

## Home: 16 pads at a glance

The home screen shows all sixteen tracks as pads, each with its sample, track type and trigger mode. The pad you hit
lights up, and the six knobs under the screen edit the selected track's volume, pan, pitch, time-stretch, loop and
trigger mode. Tempo, quantize, scene, pattern and position are always visible at the top.

<Screen name="home" alt="Home screen with 16 pads and the master meter" />

- **Track types:** Sample, Thru (the audio input through the track's effects), MIDI and instrument.
- **Trigger modes:** one-shot or gate (the sample plays only while you hold the pad), with choke groups.
- **Pad modes:** TRACK, MUTE, SLICE (one slice per pad) and SCALE (play one sample chromatically or in a scale).

## Track menu and bypass

Each track is a chain of modules you can open with one touch: playback, amp envelope, two insert effects, mixer and
LFO, plus its sequence and its recorded motion.

<Screen name="track-menu" alt="Track menu showing the module chain of a track" />

Press **BYPASS** in the header and the modules you can bypass get a dashed outline: a tap on playback, the amp
envelope or an effect now switches it off or back on instead of opening it. Bypassed modules keep their BYPASS mark
after you leave, so you always see what is switched off.

<Screen name="bypass" alt="Track menu in BYPASS mode with the delay effect bypassed" />

## Playback and amp envelope

Set the start, loop start and length on the waveform, reverse it, snap to a grid or slice it. The amp envelope shapes
each hit with attack, hold and release, velocity sensitivity and tempo sync.

## Step sequencer

Sixteen steps per bar and up to four bars per pattern. Every step can be a **full** trigger, a **half** trigger or a
**parameter-only** step, and it can lock any of the track's parameters to its own value. Add retriggers (ratchets),
micro-offsets, swing, step shift and transpose, or record in real time with quantize, pre-count and metronome.

<Screen name="sequence" alt="Sequencer with active steps, the playhead and the trigger type selector" />

## Motion

Turn on **MOTION RECORD** for a track, arm REC and play: every knob you turn, and every touch strip move, is written
into the pattern step by step. Touch strip REPEAT hits are recorded as notes too. The MOTION page shows one lane per
parameter; push the selector on a lane to zoom it to the full screen with its value scale, then draw new values with
your finger or edit a step with the selector. ADD PARAMETER lets you draw motion for a parameter you never touched,
and every edit can be undone.

## Scenes, patterns and the arranger

A project holds 16 scenes of 16 patterns each. Switch patterns and scenes while playing; switches are queued to the
end of the pass so you stay in time. Chain patterns, give each scene its own tempo, and copy, paste, swap or reset
patterns and tracks. The arranger strings scenes into a song of up to 999 sections.

<Screen name="scene-manager" alt="Scene manager with the scene and pattern grids" />

## Mixer and routing

A full mixer with volume, pan, send, mute and solo for every track, a send return and the master. The strips are wide,
each fader sits on its own live meter, and the send / master pair stays compact so the tracks get the room. Route
tracks to separate outputs, set up the headphone cue, and insert the analog-filter slot (a digital filter model, or a
loop through your own external filter).

<Screen name="mixer" alt="Mixer with 16 channel strips, two muted tracks and live meters" />

## Mute and solo

- **Solo from the pads:** hold MUTE and hit a pad to solo that track; a soloed pad breathes, and silent tracks dim.
- **MUTE MODE per track:** a mute either stops the track's sound or stops its steps, chosen in the track menu.
- **Mutes per pattern:** every pattern remembers its own mutes, so switching pattern brings back its mute set.
- **Always visible:** while the pads are in MUTE mode, a badge in the status row shows the mute mode; while MUTE is
  held, a SOLO badge.

<Screen name="home-mute" alt="Home screen in MUTE mode with the MUTE badge in the status row" />

## Effects

Two insert effects per track plus send and master effects:

| Insert effects | Send effects |
| --- | --- |
| Chorus, flanger, phaser, 2-band EQ, multi-mode filter, delay, lo-fi, compressor, distortion, ducker | Delay, reverb, and the insert effects as sends |

Every effect parameter can be locked per step and modulated by the LFO.

## Plug-in instruments (coming soon)

freesp16 is getting a **CLAP** plug-in host: load software instruments and effects onto a track and play them from
the pads and the sequencer, much like VST instruments in a computer studio. CLAP is an open plug-in standard.

## LFO

Each track has an LFO with seven shapes, free or tempo-synced, with depth and phase offset, routed to a track
parameter or an effect parameter.

<Screen name="lfo-pitch" alt="LFO screen modulating the pitch with a sine wave" />

## Touch strip

The touch strip has four modes: **PITCH** bends the selected track, **REPEAT** retriggers it at musical divisions, and
**USER1** / **USER2** sweep up to eight parameters each, each with its own range. **HOLD** keeps the last position
after you lift your finger. With REC armed, REPEAT hits are recorded onto the steps, and on tracks with MOTION RECORD
on, strip moves are recorded as [motion](#motion).

<Screen name="touch-strip" alt="Touch strip setting with the user-assignable parameters" />

## Sampling

Sample from the line or mic input with a threshold, then trim and edit on the waveform. **Live sampling** keeps a
running buffer, so you can grab the last bars after the fact and loop them in time. You can also resample the
instrument's own output, render patterns to audio and normalize.

## Library and projects

Browse samples by folder with preview, and load them onto tracks. Projects save atomically (a power cut never leaves a
half-written project), autosave in the background and resume on start.

<Screen name="browse" alt="Sample browser with folders and the preview function" />

## MIDI and sync

- MIDI tracks send notes, two CCs and program changes to external gear.
- MIDI clock in and out, and Ableton Link to sync with other apps and devices.
- Play pads and change parameters from a MIDI controller, with controller profiles and MIDI learn.

## On the Linux desktop

The same app runs natively on Linux in an 800 × 480 window, scalable up to 4×. Your computer keyboard plays the pads
and the step keys, the mouse acts as the touch screen, and audio goes through ALSA, PipeWire or PulseAudio. See
[Install on Linux](/install/linux/).
