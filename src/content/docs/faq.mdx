## What is freesp16?

A free, open-source sampler and groovebox app for the 16-pad sampler hardware built around an i.MX6 processor and an
800 × 480 touch screen, best known as the Pioneer DJ TORAIZ SP-16. It is a new app written from scratch, not a
modified copy of the stock firmware. It also runs on the Linux desktop.

## Is it official?

No. freesp16 is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Pioneer DJ or
AlphaTheta. Please don't contact them about freesp16; use the project's [issue tracker](/support/) instead.

## Can it damage my instrument?

freesp16 never flashes or rewrites the instrument's firmware. It runs from the USB key, started by the community
boot menu after the stock firmware has started, and writes its files only to the key. Removing the key and restarting
gives you the stock firmware back. As with any software, use it at your
own risk: the GPL licence comes without a warranty.

## Does it touch my existing projects and samples?

freesp16 keeps its own projects, but it can read your samples, and every save is atomic, so a power cut never leaves a
half-written project. Back up the instrument's storage before trying any new software, just in case.

## Can I open the projects I made with the stock firmware?

Yes: freesp16 imports projects made with the stock firmware, so you can keep playing your existing work.

## How do I go back to the stock firmware?

Choose the official software in the boot menu, or switch the instrument off, remove the USB key, and switch it on
again.

## Does the hardware build need an internet connection or an account?

No. Nothing to register, nothing sent anywhere.

## Why does the Linux build exist?

It's the same app, so you can try it, prepare projects on your computer, or develop and test without the instrument.

## Which firmware files do I need?

None. freesp16 doesn't include or need any part of the stock firmware, and the project never distributes it.

## Is it really free?

Yes, free as in freedom and free of charge. The source code is under the GPL-3.0-or-later; the JUCE framework it is
built with is under the AGPLv3. You can study, change and share it under those licences.

## What is the boot menu?

The [boot menu](https://github.com/freesp16/bootmenu) is the `autoexec.bin` on the USB key: a small community
launcher that lists freesp16, other community software on the key and the official software, and starts the one you
choose. It remembers your choice, so after the first time freesp16 starts on its own.

On the [freesp16 Discord server](https://discord.gg/DedpwtE6EK).
