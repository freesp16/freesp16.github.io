## Made for the TORAIZ SP-16

freesp16 runs on the **Pioneer DJ TORAIZ SP-16**. It is written from scratch for this instrument: its quad-core ARM
Cortex-A9 processor, its GPU, its 800 × 480 touch screen and every control on its panel. The 16 velocity-sensitive pads,
the six knobs, the selector, the 16 step keys, the touch strip and all the lamps work as you expect.

<figure class="not-content hardware">
	<Image src={hardware} alt="Illustration of the TORAIZ SP-16 panel running freesp16 on its screen" widths={[800, 1400, 2000]} sizes="(min-width: 72rem) 68rem, 100vw" />
	<figcaption>freesp16 on the TORAIZ SP-16 (illustration).</figcaption>
</figure>

You don't flash anything to install it: copy one file to a USB key, plug it into your SP-16, and freesp16 starts.
Power-cycle without the key and your SP-16 starts its stock firmware as usual.

## Fast and smooth

freesp16 is tuned to get the most out of your SP-16, so the screen feels fluid and the sound never suffers.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="A smooth 60 fps screen" icon="rocket">
		The screen is drawn by the SP-16's graphics chip, so menus, meters and waveforms move smoothly at 60 frames per
		second.
	</Card>
	<Card title="More power for your sound" icon="analytics">
		Because graphics are handled separately, the processor stays free for audio: more voices and more effects at the
		same time.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Instant response" icon="approve-check-circle">
		Hit a pad and you hear it right away. freesp16 is built for low latency, so playing feels tight.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Everything a groovebox needs

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="16 pads, 16 tracks" icon="puzzle">
		Sample, Thru, MIDI and instrument tracks with region, loop, reverse, pitch, time-stretch, choke groups and
		velocity-sensitive pads. Play them as tracks, mutes, slices or in a musical scale.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Step sequencer" icon="list-format">
		Full, half and parameter-only triggers, per-step parameter locks, retrigger, offset, swing, real-time recording
		with quantize and pre-count.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Scenes, patterns and songs" icon="random">
		16 scenes × 16 patterns, queued switching and chaining while playing, per-scene tempo, and an arranger for
		songs of up to 999 sections.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Effects" icon="star">
		Two insert slots per track, send and master effects: chorus, flanger, phaser, EQ, multi-mode filter, delay,
		reverb, lo-fi, distortion, compressor and ducker, all lockable per step.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Modulation and performance" icon="analytics">
		A per-track LFO with seven shapes, amp envelopes, and a touch strip with pitch, repeat and two user-assignable
		modes with HOLD.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sampling and sync" icon="setting">
		Sample from the inputs, live-sample from a ring buffer, resample and render. MIDI tracks, MIDI clock in and out,
		and Ableton Link.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
